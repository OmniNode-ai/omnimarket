Reading the manifest

Reports

Every report here is drawn from data files that nodes write (or, for a snapshot, that a lane wrote), and every card shows how old the data behind it is. A source that could not be read says so; it is never shown as a quiet zero. This site is on the lab server and answers on the tailnet only.

Refreshed data on the cards is checked against the clock in this browser. A live source older than 90 minutes is amber and older than 6 hours is red. A snapshot is never red, because nothing refreshes it; its card says what its age is measured from.
