How the factory's day-to-day work has gone since 15 October, with the factory reset marked, and a comparison of the hours before the reset with the same number of hours after it. Quality comes first; busyness and cost are context.

Reading the series.

The series is computed by the process-health node from the work log, the landing controller's records, the repository status events, the merge history and the Linear snapshot. Quality measures come first; merges, delegation and cost are context, kept in a separate grey block. A source that could not be read, or only partly, is shown as unread or partial and its measure is never drawn as a zero. A hollow point on a chart is a day whose sources were only partly read.
