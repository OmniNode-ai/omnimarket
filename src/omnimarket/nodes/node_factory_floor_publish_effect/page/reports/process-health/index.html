Process over time

Choose a range to see how the factory's work changed. Each measure compares that range with the same length of time immediately before it. Quality comes first; busyness and cost are context.

Reading the series.

The series is computed by the process-health node from the work log, the landing controller's records, the repository status events, the merge history and the Linear snapshot. Quality measures come first; merges, delegation and cost are context, kept in a separate grey block. A source that could not be read, or only partly, is shown as unread or partial and its measure is never drawn as a zero. Gaps in a line mean unmeasured. Tap the graph or move the inspection slider to read a point. All displayed times are Eastern; custom times use Eastern even when your device uses another zone.
