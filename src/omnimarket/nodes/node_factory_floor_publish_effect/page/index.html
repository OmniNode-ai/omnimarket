Lean Factory Floor

Every plan is a car on its own assembly line. Each station is a stage or wave, and the car is built part by part as stations finish. Feeder lines under a station are the work that supplies it. Bays are the lab machines doing the work. The page reads one JSON record, written by the factory floor nodes and published on every tick.
