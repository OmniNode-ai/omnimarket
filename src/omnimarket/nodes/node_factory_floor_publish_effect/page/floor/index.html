Lean Factory Floor

Every plan is a car on its own assembly line. Each station is a stage or wave, and the car is built part by part as stations finish. Feeder lines under a station are the work that supplies it. Bays are the machines doing the work, with the lanes placed on each. Everything below is read from the factory record and the process-health series, published on every tick; a source that could not be read is drawn as unread.

Sources

    The record is computed by the factory floor nodes from the plans and their task manifests, the pull request watcher, the rolling work ledger, the Linear snapshot, the lab placement reading and the friction report. The page reloads it every minute and shows how old it is. Nothing on the page is typed in: a figure that has no source is drawn unread, never as a zero.
