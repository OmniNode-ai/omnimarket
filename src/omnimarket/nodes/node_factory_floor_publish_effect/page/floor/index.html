What the record was computed from

Sources

Where each figure comes from and how fresh it is.

    The record is computed by the factory floor nodes from the plans and their task manifests, the change watcher, the work log, the ticket snapshot, the lab placement reading and the friction report. The page reloads it every minute and shows how old it is. Nothing on the page is typed in: a figure that has no source is drawn unread, never as a zero.
